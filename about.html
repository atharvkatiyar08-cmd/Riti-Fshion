02 COMPANY INTRODUCTION

Your garment
manufacturing partner.

RITI FASHION provides garment manufacturing and stitching job-work services in Surat, Gujarat, for B2B brands, D2C brands, online sellers, clothing and fashion brands, wholesalers, startups and growing apparel businesses.

Customers provide the fabric. Support includes pattern and sample development, cutting, stitching, finishing and packaging, based on the agreed garment requirements.
