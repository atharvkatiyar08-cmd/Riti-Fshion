01 GARMENT MANUFACTURING PROCESS

A clear path from brief
to dispatch.

Each production plan is agreed against the garment requirements, sample approval and quotation. No production timeline is assumed until the order scope is reviewed.

  1. 01

    Requirement & Design Discussion

    Every manufacturing project begins with understanding the brand's requirements. We discuss garment category, design references, specifications, expected quantities, fabric requirements and finishing.

    Key activities

    • Understanding the clothing brand's requirements.
    • Reviewing design references and garment specifications.
    • Discussing fabric and material requirements.
    • Understanding expected quantities.
    • Identifying construction and finishing expectations.
    • Discussing sampling and production requirements.
  2. 02

    Pattern & Sample Development

    Once the requirements are understood, the design moves into development. A physical sample is prepared to evaluate construction, appearance, fit and finishing before larger production begins.

    Key activities

    • Reviewing the design and reference.
    • Preparing pattern and construction details as required.
    • Developing the initial garment sample.
    • Evaluating fit and construction.
    • Reviewing stitching and finishing.
    • Making agreed adjustments and preparing the sample for approval.
  3. 03

    Sample Review & Approval

    Sample approval establishes the standard that production must follow. The brand reviews the garment and provides feedback. Required adjustments are discussed before the project moves forward.

    Key activities

    • Reviewing the completed sample.
    • Checking garment appearance and construction.
    • Evaluating fit and finishing.
    • Discussing feedback and modifications.
    • Confirming approved specifications.
    • Establishing the production reference.
  4. 04

    Production Planning & Stitching

    After approval, the order is organised around the agreed requirements, specifications and quantities. Garment stitching is carried out with attention to consistency and construction.

    Key activities

    • Confirming approved production requirements.
    • Organising materials according to the agreed scope.
    • Planning the production workflow.
    • Coordinating garment stitching.
    • Maintaining consistency across the batch.
    • Monitoring progress and communicating relevant updates.
  5. 05

    Quality Checking & Finishing

    Quality is considered throughout production rather than only at the end. Completed garments are reviewed for stitching consistency, construction and finishing against the approved sample.

    Key activities

    • Reviewing stitching consistency.
    • Checking garment construction.
    • Inspecting seams and finishing.
    • Comparing garments with the approved sample.
    • Identifying issues requiring correction.
    • Completing final garment preparation.
  6. 06

    Packaging & Delivery

    The final stage prepares completed garments for dispatch. Finishing and packaging follow the requirements agreed with the client, and the order is organised for handover or delivery according to the agreed arrangement.

    Key activities

    • Final garment preparation.
    • Folding and presentation.
    • Packing according to agreed requirements.
    • Order arrangement.
    • Final coordination.
    • Dispatch or delivery handover.

The order scope and timing depend on the approved garment requirements and agreed production quotation; no fixed lead time is promised.
