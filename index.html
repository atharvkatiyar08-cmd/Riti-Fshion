05 COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
start a brief.

Practical details for apparel businesses considering a manufacturing enquiry.

What is the current minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on the design and production requirements. Contact RITI FASHION for confirmation of the current MOQ before placing an order.

Does RITI FASHION supply fabric?

Fabric is customer-provided. Fabric sourcing is not offered. Share the material details when discussing your production brief.

Can you develop a sample before bulk production?

Pattern and sample development can be discussed using your specifications, a reference photograph or an existing garment. The sample and requirements are reviewed before production is quoted.

Do you accept international enquiries?

RITI FASHION is based in Surat, India, and can discuss delivery outside India. Destination availability and delivery terms are agreed directly for each order.

Can I buy sample pieces on this website?

The RITI Edit is being prepared; no verified products or online orders are available yet. When purchasing opens, payment will be online only with no cash on delivery.
