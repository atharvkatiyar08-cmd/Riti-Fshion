01 PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy,
clearly explained.

This policy explains how information is handled when you contact RITI FASHION through this website or choose to use a direct communication channel.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Information you may provide

The production-enquiry form asks for contact details and information about a garment brief, including category, service, estimated quantity and specifications. Optional fields include business name, materials, design details and timing.

If reference files or photographs are provided by another approved future enquiry method, visitors should share only materials they are authorised to provide and should avoid sending confidential or third-party personal information. The current public form does not accept file attachments. Do not include payment-card or banking credentials in an enquiry.

If you choose to contact RITI FASHION by email, telephone or WhatsApp, the information you provide is handled by the service you use and may be received by RITI FASHION when you send or complete the communication.

Website enquiry form

Website form submission is currently unavailable. Information typed into the form stays in your browser and is not sent or stored by the website. WhatsApp opens a message draft for you to review; it is sent only if you choose to send it. Email, telephone and map links open the relevant service only when selected.

Information you choose to send directly to RITI FASHION may be used to respond to your request, discuss garment requirements, prepare a quotation and communicate about a potential or agreed manufacturing engagement.

Cookies, fonts and other services

The public pages use locally stored fonts and images and do not currently include an analytics service. WhatsApp, email, telephone and map links open third-party services only when you choose to use them; those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Orders, payments and delivery

No sample products are currently listed for sale. The website does not accept sample orders or payments and does not collect order, delivery or payment details. When sample purchasing is introduced, payment will be online only; cash on delivery will not be offered.

Before online orders open, this policy will identify the relevant order and payment arrangements and explain how any associated information is handled. Delivery terms will be shown with confirmed product listings.

Data sharing

Information is shared with RITI FASHION only when you choose to send it through a direct communication channel. Third-party communications providers may process information under their own policies. RITI FASHION does not sell personal information. No sample-order information is currently collected.

Security and retention

Information entered into the unavailable website form is not transmitted or saved by the website. Messages or approved future attachments sent directly to RITI FASHION or a third-party service may be retained according to the applicable business and record-keeping requirements. A fixed retention period has not been confirmed. When website enquiry storage is activated, the applicable storage location and retention period should be published here before collection begins.

Your privacy rights

You may contact RITI FASHION to ask about information you have shared, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements. For information held by a third-party provider, you may also need to contact that provider.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information shared with RITI FASHION, email ritifashion.surat@gmail.com or call +91 63547 59740.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if the website's contact or ordering options change in a way that affects personal information.
