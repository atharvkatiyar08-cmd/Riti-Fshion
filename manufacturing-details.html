07 FABRIC & MATERIAL HANDLING

fabric and materials.

Fabric is supplied by the customer; fabric sourcing is not provided. Share available fabric details, construction requirements and agreed accessories with the garment brief so they can be considered during planning.

11 QUOTATION PROCESS

Costing follows
the approved sample.

  1. Customer shares garment requirements.
  2. Design and specifications are reviewed.
  3. Sample is developed and evaluated.
  4. Final costing is determined against the agreed brief.
  5. A production quotation is provided for approval.

Every garment has its own specifications. Final costing is determined after sample development and evaluation of the production requirements. The total production volume and garment complexity also influence the final quotation.

Costing also depends on garment design, construction complexity, fabric characteristics, accessories, finishing and other agreed requirements. No fixed garment prices are published.
